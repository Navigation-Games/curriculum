# Frameworks

The Navigation Games curriculum draws on orienteering-specific frameworks from OUSA and our own progression, as well as broader educational frameworks.

### Orienteering Frameworks

- **[Orienteering Development Model](orienteering-development-model)** - OUSA's five-stage framework for developing orienteers, based on the TeamUSA American Development Model
- **[OUSA Skill Level Recognition Program](ousa-skill-levels)** - OUSA's four-level progression of concrete orienteering skills, from Basic through Expert
- **[Navigation Games Progression](skill-concept-sequence)** - Our ordered sequence of 48 orienteering concepts and skills, cross-referenced to the ODM and OUSA Skill Levels

### Education and Ethics Frameworks

- **[PE Standards](pe-standards)** - Alignment with the 2024 SHAPE America National Physical Education Standards
- **[Experiential Learning](experiential-learning)** - Learning by doing: the cycle of experience, reflection, and application
- **[Social-Emotional Learning](social-emotional-learning)** - How orienteering develops independence, resilience, collaboration, and other SEL competencies (CASEL framework)
- **[SEL Program Quality Assessment](sel-pqa)** - Mapping the curriculum to the Forum for Youth Investment's SEL PQA observation rubric, used by ACA and other program evaluators
- **[Leave No Trace](leave-no-trace)** - Outdoor ethics and orienteering's relationship to trail use
- **[MA Vision of a Graduate](ma-vision-of-a-graduate)** - Alignment with the Massachusetts Vision of a Graduate's six skills and competencies

# Navigation Games Kits

Navigation Games offers instructional kits containing materials for four core activities. The kits were developed from years of working with Cambridge Public Schools and other educational settings.

## What's in the Kit

Each kit includes materials for:
- **Animal-O** -- streamers with animal pictures, clue sheets
- **Geometric-O** -- pattern maps and colored landmark cones
- **Basketball-O** -- court maps for indoor orienteering
- **Clothespin-O** -- clothespins for checkpoint confirmation

The kit also includes activity descriptions covering the progression from basic boundary and exploration exercises through map-based orienteering, plus background on orienteering pedagogy and connections to national PE standards.

Items marked with the <span className="ng-kit-badge"><img src="/curriculum/img/ng-logo-icon.svg" alt="" />NG Kit</span> badge on the [Materials Index](../materials) page are included in the kit.

## Getting a Kit

Kits are available through [Navigation Games](https://www.navigationgames.org). Contact Navigation Games for current pricing and availability.

## Using the Kit with This Curriculum

The kit materials align directly with the first four activities in the [Grade 3-5 lesson sequence](/lessons/school/grade-3-5/):

| Kit Activity | Curriculum Lessons |
|---|---|
| Animal-O | Lessons 2-3 (Explore & Find, Clue Sheets) |
| Geometric-O | Lesson 4 (Map Readiness) |
| Basketball-O | Lesson A (Indoor Orienteering) |
| Clothespin-O | K-2 Lesson 3 |

For Score-O, Map Walk, and Point-to-Point (Lessons 5-6), you will need orienteering maps of your site. See [Maps](../maps) for how to get one.

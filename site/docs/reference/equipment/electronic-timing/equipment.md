# Equipment for Electronic Timing

To build one complete set for teaching orienteering with electronic timing, schools buy:

- **SPORTident school kit** from [sportident.us](https://www.sportident.us/product-page/school-and-training-set), and 13 [mounting holders](https://www.sportident.us/product-page/mounting-holder) that fit the SI boxes in the kit. The kit cost about $2,800 in August 2025 and has gone up since because of tariffs. SPORTident USA can take a credit card or give you a quote so you can obtain a purchase order. They ship the kit with an invoice. If kits are out of stock, the whole process can take 4 to 6 weeks.
- **13 traffic cones with holes in the top.** We recommend 12-inch cones ([example](https://www.amazon.com/dp/B0C7SR23YR)) because they're easier to carry. 18-inch cones ([example](https://www.amazon.com/Traffic-Collars-Parking-Control-Construction/dp/B0CPP3NFBT/)) are sturdier but harder to carry.
- **A hardwood dowel** that fits into the top of the cones, and screws to attach the mounting holders (see [Cone toppers](#cone-toppers) below).
- **A Windows computer or tablet** to run EasyGecNG. Pick one with good battery life for use outdoors. See [Installing EasyGecNG](../installing).
- **A [Navigation Games kit](https://www.navigationgames.org/product-page/beginning-orienteering-instruction-kit)** with the laminated materials (animal pictures, clue sheets, maps). See [Navigation Games Kits](/reference/equipment/kits).

## What's in the SPORTident school kit

<Pic src="/img/electronic-timing/si-boxes.png" alt="SI boxes: checkpoints 31 to 40, Start, Finish, Clear, and the mini reader" width={300} />

- **SI boxes** numbered 31 to 40 for the checkpoints, plus Start, Finish and Clear boxes. The Clear box is also the **SI-Master**, used to synchronize the other boxes' clocks (see [Maintaining the SI Boxes](../maintenance)).
- **A mini reader** (also called the download box) that plugs into the computer's USB port and reads the SI cards.
- **30 SI cards** and 30 Velcro strips to attach to them.

Maintenance also uses a coupling stick and a Service/OFF card (see [Maintaining the SI Boxes](../maintenance)).

## Putting it together

1. Make the cone toppers with the dowels, screws and mounting holders.
2. Attach the Velcro strips to the SI cards.
3. Install the software on the computer ([Installing EasyGecNG](../installing)).

## Cone toppers

Navigation Games uses cone toppers to hold SI boxes on traffic cones, indoors and outdoors.

1. Cut a wooden dowel into 3-inch lengths. Use a hardwood like oak or maple, not pine, so the screws hold better in the end grain. The dowel should fit snugly into the top of your cones without being hard to push in.
2. Screw the black SI box mounting holders into the ends of the dowels. SPORTident USA sells the holders. Their website may not show everything they offer, so contact them if you don't see it.

If you'd rather not make your own, you can buy [cone tops for SI boxes from O-Store in Canada](https://www.o-store.ca/en/cone-tops-for-si), or Navigation Games can make cone toppers for you. [Contact us](mailto:admin@navigationgames.org) for a quote.

## You might also want

- An Android phone or tablet with SPORTident's apps, to read SI cards and print results without a Windows computer. This matters less for elementary school PE than running EasyGecNG.
- Other orienteering supplies from [Berman's O Supply](https://bermansosupply.com/) or [The Compass Store](https://www.thecompassstore.com/).

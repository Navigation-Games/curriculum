# Activities & Lesson Plans

Everything in the curricula at a glance.

## Activities

### Core Activities

- [**Boundary Run**](/activities/core/boundary-run) - Travel the boundary of the play area
- [**Gathering**](/activities/core/gathering) - Return to the teacher on a signal
- [**Clothespin-O**](/activities/core/clothespin-o) - Find hidden objects and describe their locations
- [**Animal-O**](/activities/core/animal-o) - Use a clue sheet to find checkpoints in order
- [**Geometric-O**](/activities/core/geometric-o) - Use a simple map to do progressively harder courses
- [**Symbol-O**](/activities/core/symbol-o) - Understand map symbols and the features they represent
- [**Map Walk**](/activities/core/map-walk) - Walk a route together, matching map symbols to real features
- [**Score-O**](/activities/core/score-o) - Visit as many checkpoints as possible in any order
- [**Poison-O**](/activities/core/poison-o) - Visit only the checkpoints shown on your map
- [**Point-to-Point**](/activities/core/point-to-point) - Complete a course using an orienteering map

### Camp and Additional Activities

- [**Map Discussion**](/activities/core/map-discussion) - Discuss how orienteering maps represent the world
- [**Symbol Relay**](/activities/core/symbol-relay) - Team relay to learn map symbols competitively
- [**Compass Basics**](/activities/core/compass-basics) - Use a compass to go in the correct direction
- [**Line-O**](/activities/core/line-o) - Follow the line and find unmarked checkpoints along the way
- [**Star Relay**](/activities/core/star-relay) - Team up; hand off between checkpoints
- [**Checkpoint Copy Relay**](/activities/core/checkpoint-copy-relay) - Relay-race format to practice map reading and simplification
- [**Window-O**](/activities/core/window-o) - Navigate with parts of the map removed
- [**Find Your Way Home**](/activities/core/find-your-way-home) - Navigate back to home base from an unfamiliar starting point
- [**Draw the Space**](/activities/core/draw-the-space) - Draw a map of the space from above

### Activities Without Pages Yet

- **Explore & Find** - Find checkpoints within a defined boundary
- **Reverse Score-O** - Find controls and mark their locations on the map
- **Basketball-O** - Orienteering on a basketball court map
- **Relay Race** - Teams compete in relay format

## School Lesson Plans

### Grade K-2

- [**1 - Safety and Space**](/lessons/school/grade-k-2/lesson-1) - Here is where you can go; come back when you hear the signal
- [**2 - Explore and Find**](/lessons/school/grade-k-2/lesson-2) - Explore to find animal checkpoints
- [**3 - Treasure Hunt**](/lessons/school/grade-k-2/lesson-3) - Search for hidden treasures and describe where you found them
- [**4 - Clue Sheets**](/lessons/school/grade-k-2/lesson-4) - Follow a clue sheet to find checkpoints in order
- [**5 - Draw a Map**](/lessons/school/grade-k-2/lesson-5) - Draw the space from above
- [**6 - Read a Map**](/lessons/school/grade-k-2/lesson-6) - Use a simple map to find checkpoints

### Grade 3-5

- [**1 - Boundary & Explore**](/lessons/school/grade-3-5/lesson-1) - Establish the play area, practice the gathering signal, and explore to find checkpoints
- [**2 - Clue Sheets**](/lessons/school/grade-3-5/lesson-2) - Follow a clue sheet to visit checkpoints in order
- [**3 - Map Readiness**](/lessons/school/grade-3-5/lesson-3) - Bridge from clue sheets to simple maps
- [**4 - Map Symbols**](/lessons/school/grade-3-5/lesson-4) - Symbols tell you what features are on the ground
- [**5 - Map Checkpoints**](/lessons/school/grade-3-5/lesson-5) - Navigate using a map, visit checkpoints in any order
- [**6 - Point-to-Point**](/lessons/school/grade-3-5/lesson-6) - Navigate a course visiting checkpoints in order
- [**A - Indoor Orienteering**](/lessons/school/grade-3-5/a-indoor-orienteering) - Orienteering activities adapted for a gym or indoor space

### Grade 6+

- [**1 - Clue Sheets and Maps**](/lessons/school/grade-6-plus/lesson-1) - Accelerated introduction for older students
- [**2 - Map Reading**](/lessons/school/grade-6-plus/lesson-2) - Map symbols, features, and guided navigation
- [**3 - Strategic Navigation**](/lessons/school/grade-6-plus/lesson-3) - Score-O with route planning and strategy
- [**4 - Reverse Mapping**](/lessons/school/grade-6-plus/lesson-4) - Find checkpoints and mark their locations on the map
- [**5 - Orienteering Courses**](/lessons/school/grade-6-plus/lesson-5) - Point-to-point courses with full map reading
- [**6 - Racing**](/lessons/school/grade-6-plus/lesson-6) - Competitive orienteering in relay and individual formats

## Camp Lesson Plans

### Camp Intro (3 sessions)

Three standalone activities for camps just getting started with orienteering.

- [**1 - Animal-O**](/lessons/camp/intro/lesson-1) - Clue sheet orienteering
- [**2 - Find Your Way Home**](/lessons/camp/intro/lesson-2) - Navigate back to home base
- [**3 - Map Treasure Hunt**](/lessons/camp/intro/lesson-3) - Score-O with orienteering maps

### Camp Skill Development (6 sessions)

Fundamentals of orienteering map navigation in six 70-minute sessions over two weeks.

- [**1 - Getting Started**](/lessons/camp/full/lesson-1) - Safety, Animal-O, Symbol Relay, Map Discussion
- [**2 - Reading the Map**](/lessons/camp/full/lesson-2) - Map Walk and Score-O
- [**3 - Compass and Courses**](/lessons/camp/full/lesson-3) - Compass Basics and orienteering courses
- [**4 - Precision and Teamwork**](/lessons/camp/full/lesson-4) - Line-O and Star Relay
- [**5 - Compass Challenge**](/lessons/camp/full/lesson-5) - Compass Segments and Poison-O
- [**6 - Courses and Relays**](/lessons/camp/full/lesson-6) - Full courses and team relays

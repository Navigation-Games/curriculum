# Activities & Lesson Plans

Everything in the curricula at a glance.

## Activities

- [**Boundary Run**](/activities/core/boundary-run) - Travel the boundary of the play area.
- [**Gathering**](/activities/core/gathering) - Return to the teacher on a signal.
- [**Animal-O**](/activities/core/animal-o) - Use a clue sheet to find checkpoints in order.
- [**Geometric-O**](/activities/core/geometric-o) - Use a simple map to do progressively harder courses.
- [**Map Walk**](/activities/core/map-walk) - Walk a route together, matching map symbols to real features.
- [**Score-O**](/activities/core/score-o) - Visit as many checkpoints as possible in any order.

## Grade 3-5 Lesson Plans

- [**1 - Boundary**](/lessons/school/grade-3-5/boundary) - Establish the play area and practice the gathering signal.
- [**2 - Return**](/lessons/school/grade-3-5/return) - Go out, find something, and come back on the signal.
- [**3 - Explore & Find**](/lessons/school/grade-3-5/explore-and-find) - There are things out there; go find them and come back.
- [**4 - Clue Sheets**](/lessons/school/grade-3-5/clue-sheets) - Follow a clue sheet to visit checkpoints in order.
- [**5 - Map Readiness**](/lessons/school/grade-3-5/map-readiness) - Bridge from clue sheets to simple maps.
- [**6 - Maps**](/lessons/school/grade-3-5/maps) - Navigate with a real orienteering map.
- [**A - Indoor Orienteering**](/lessons/school/grade-3-5/a-indoor-orienteering) - Orienteering activities adapted for a gym or indoor space.

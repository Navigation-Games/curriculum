# Using SI Equipment (Electronic Timing)

Setup and usage instructions for the SPORTident electronic timing system. *(Content coming soon.)*

## Quick Reference

1. **Clear** your SI card at the CLEAR checkpoint (yellow sign)
2. **Start** by punching the START checkpoint (green sign). Your time begins
3. **Visit** each checkpoint in order, punching the SI box at each one. Listen for the beep
4. **Finish** by punching the FINISH checkpoint. Your time stops
5. **Download** at the computer table to see your time and check your course

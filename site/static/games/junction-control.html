Junction Control

Teams race to visit every checkpoint, traveling only on trails. The team with the most players at an intersection blocks it, and players who arrive outnumbered are held until the blocking team lets them go. When a team has every checkpoint, everyone heads for a bus stop on the edge of the map. The first team with everyone on a bus wins.

    How the simulator plays

    • The map is a random trail network about 900 by 620 meters. Players run 2.5 to 3.4 meters a second, and pairs and packs move at their slowest member's speed.
    • Each pair or pack heads for the nearest checkpoint that no other group on its team is going for. A team always knows which checkpoints it has (the team channel).
    • "Everyone alone" sends each player off on their own. "Blockers + pairs" parks one or two players at the busiest intersections to hold other teams up, and sends the rest out in pairs.
    • When the blocking team picks the way out, it sends the held player toward whichever trail leads farthest from where that player is trying to go.
    • With rescue on, if one more teammate would tie the count, the nearest free teammate within 350 meters comes to help.
    • A wrong turn sends a player down a different trail than they meant to take. They notice at the next intersection and replan.
    • Once a team has every checkpoint, each player heads for whichever bus they can catch soonest, at any stop. The team finishes when its last player's bus leaves. A player who gets held on the way can miss a bus and hold the whole team up.
    • Not in the simulator: talk between teams, deals, the game operator's announcements, players leaving the trails, or anyone staying lost for longer than one wrong turn.

# Kickstart Orienteering: Put Your School on the Map

Kickstart Orienteering is Navigation Games' program for teachers and school leaders who want to bring orienteering to their school. Your students play navigation games, learn to read a map of their school grounds or a nearby park, and get moving outdoors.

**[Apply for Kickstart Orienteering](https://docs.google.com/forms/d/e/1FAIpQLSfsEDt3EpRNvK7MgOFHE-PENPIE7vbJFl2B6eF7yCfhp1Hcsg/viewform)** (about five minutes). Questions? Email admin@navigationgames.org.

## What your school gets

- **Online training.** Eight short, self-paced units. For each unit, you have some readings and sometimes videos. Then you complete assignments through an interactive conversation with our course tutor. With the tutor's help, you'll submit an assignment that Navigation Games staff read and respond to. The course may count toward professional development hours, if your district or managers allow it. Here's a [one-page course description](/pd-credit/) to give whoever approves your PD hours.
- **Two consultations** with Navigation Games by video call: one to plan your unit before you teach, and one afterwards to look at how it went and plan next year.
- **A Beginning Orienteering Instruction Kit**, with everything you need for the first lessons, which don't need a map.
- **An orienteering map** of your school grounds or a nearby park, made through Orienteering USA's Youth Map Program (YMP), if you need one. The tutor helps you with the application to Orienteering USA.
- **The [Navigation Games curriculum](/)**: lesson plans for every grade, and the lesson planning advisor.
- **A site visit** from Navigation Games staff, where we have staff or partners nearby.

## What you commit to

- Apply for a map through YMP, if you need one, and help the mapper check the draft (up to 3 hours)
- Complete the online training and the planning survey
- Teach orienteering to your students
- Complete a short survey after you teach

## How it works

1. **Apply.** The application takes about five minutes.
2. **Start the online training.** We email you when you're enrolled. Unit 1 helps you apply for your map. Maps can take several months, so it's good to apply early.
3. **Plan your unit.** Units 2 to 5 take about 4 hours in all. Unit 5 ends with your unit plan and the planning survey.
4. **Get your kit, and meet with us.** We ship the kit once you've finished Unit 5 and sent the planning survey, and we schedule the first consultation.
5. **Teach.** Start with the kit activities, which don't need a map, and move to your school map when it's ready. Units 6 and 7 help you design a first course and reflect after each class.
6. **Look ahead.** The second consultation and Unit 8 help you plan the next year.

## Costs

| | Price |
|---|---|
| Online training, including both consultations | $185 |
| Beginning Orienteering Instruction Kit | $250 (may rise to $300 in 2027) |
| Map of your school, Navigation Games' share | $300 (Orienteering USA's YMP often pays about half the cost of a first map) |
| Site visit | $1,000 |

You only pay for what you need. If you want to print and laminate materials yourself, you won't need a kit. If you are teaching young children and won't need a map, you can leave that cost out. The site visit is optional.

There are several ways to pay for it, from your school's education foundation to your PD budget. See [Funding for Kickstart Orienteering](/kickstart-funding/). Navigation Games may have grant funding available for some schools.

## Who we are

[Navigation Games](https://www.navigationgames.org/) is a nonprofit based in Cambridge, Massachusetts, with a goal of bringing orienteering to every child. Since 2015 we've run orienteering programs for schools, camps and youth organizations. Our curriculum was developed with dozens of educators and orienteering coaches, and tested with thousands of children in the Boston area.

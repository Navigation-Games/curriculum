## Grade 3-5 Progression

Six lessons building through increasing levels of abstraction:

| Lesson | Focus | Key Activities |
|---|---|---|
| 1. Explore | Safety, boundaries, gathering, observation, describing spaces | Boundary Run, Gathering, Explore & Find |
| 2. Clue Sheets | Sequential navigation with picture codes, confirming you are at the correct checkpoint | Animal-O |
| 3. Patterns | First use of maps, orienting the map with landmarks or spatial relationships of objects on the map | Geometric-O |
| 4. Map Introduction | Orienteering maps, symbols, map walking, relocation | Map Discussion, Map Walk, Symbol-O |
| 5. Map Checkpoints | Finding and placing checkpoints on real maps | Score-O, Poison-O, Place Your Own Checkpoints |
| 6. Point-to-Point Navigation | Following a course in order, route choice | Orienteering Courses |

Plus an [Indoor Orienteering](/lessons/grade-3-5/a-indoor-orienteering) alternative for gym settings.

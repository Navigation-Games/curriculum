Fun and educational orienteering activities for camp settings. Choose a quick intro or a full multi-session progression.

## Skill Development Curriculum (6 Sessions)

A two-week program with six 70-minute sessions, modeled on the Camp Belknap program. Each session starts with a safety review and builds on previous skills.

### Week 1: Introduction

| Session | Activities |
|---|---|
| 1 | Safety, Animal-O, Symbol Relay, Description Relay, Map Discussion |
| 2 | Safety, Map Walk, Score-O |
| 3 | Safety, Compass Basics, Courses |

### Week 2: Development

| Session | Activities |
|---|---|
| 4 | Safety, Symbol Relay, Line-O, Star Relay |
| 5 | Safety, Compass Segments, Poison-O |
| 6 | Safety, Courses/Relays |

Culminates in an **All-Camp Team Treasure Hunt (Score-O)**.

## Description

Compass Basics introduces students to using a compass for orienteering. Students learn that the compass needle always points to magnetic north, practice identifying cardinal directions, and use the compass to orient their map. The activity progresses from finding directions to setting the map to taking bearings from a course on the map.


## Goals

- Short: Directional awareness
  Long: Develop directional awareness using a compass

- Short: Direction from you
  Long: Work out which direction something is from you


## Vocabulary

- Direction
  A compass direction such as north, south, east, west, or any combination (northeast, southwest, etc.).

- Magnetic north
  The direction the compass needle points. The north lines on an orienteering map are aligned to magnetic north.


## Setup

1. No physical setup is needed. Have compasses and maps ready to hand out.


## Steps

**1. Direction.** Discuss how a compass always points to magnetic north. Teach a silly acronym for the cardinal directions. Ask students to point in various directions by using their compass (e.g. north, west, southeast).

**2. Map setting.** Give out maps and demonstrate how to orient the map using the north lines. Turn the students various ways and get them to orient the map to north.

**3. Bearings.** Ask the students to pretend they are standing at the start triangle and to take a bearing as if they are going to control 1. Ask them to point in the direction of travel. Check each student is pointing the correct way. Repeat for each control.


## Delivery

1. Introduce the compass and magnetic north; practice pointing in directions
2. Hand out maps; demonstrate orienting the map using north lines
3. Practice taking bearings from the map and pointing in the direction of travel


## Reflection

- How did you ensure you were facing the right direction?
- What is special about the colors on the compass needle?
- How could a compass be helpful when orienteering?
- Did the compass work differently when held horizontally and vertically?


## Extensions

- Show students how to take bearings using the compass swivel housing
- Practice walking, jogging, or running in a straight line while staying on the correct bearing

## What the course is

Orienteering is a sport in which students use a map to find checkpoints, choosing their own route. This course prepares teachers, especially PE teachers, to teach Navigation Games' free orienteering curriculum (curriculum.navigationgames.org) to their own students, indoors or outdoors. It includes teaching at least one orienteering lesson with students.

## Learning outcomes

By the end of the course, the teacher can:

1. Explain orienteering and its benefits for students in plain language
2. Order the curriculum's progression from pre-map skills to map skills, and explain why
3. Run an orienteering activity safely, starting with a clear safety briefing
4. Write an orienteering unit plan that fits their students, space and schedule
5. Design an orienteering course on their school's map
6. Reflect on their teaching and adapt it

## Alignment

The lessons align with the SHAPE America National Physical Education Standards (2024). Each lesson plan lists the grade-level indicators it addresses. Orienteering also builds social-emotional skills (CASEL) such as self-management, decision-making and working with a partner.

## Welcome

This course gets you ready to teach orienteering to your own students. It's part of Kickstart Orienteering, Navigation Games' program for schools. Plan on about 4 hours before your kit ships, spread over two or three weeks, then three more short units while you teach, mostly reflections on how your classes went. With the two consultations and the class you teach, that comes to about 10 contact hours.

## How a unit works

Each unit is a short page followed by a conversation with the course tutor, an AI assistant. The tutor asks about your situation and helps you make something you'll actually use, like a pitch for your principal or the safety briefing you'll give your class. You sign in once, with Google or with a link we email you, and then you can stop partway and pick up later on any device.

You can talk instead of typing. Your computer or phone already has voice typing (Windows key + H on Windows, the Fn or Globe key twice on a Mac, the microphone on a phone keyboard), and it's often quicker, especially when the tutor asks about your school and students. You can also listen to the tutor: press Listen under any of its messages. To hear a whole page read aloud, use your browser: in Edge, press Ctrl+Shift+U (or right-click the page and choose Read aloud); in Chrome, right-click and choose Open in reading mode, then press play.

When the tutor writes up your finished piece, Navigation Games staff read it and get back to you, usually within 3 business days.

## Your cohort

Teachers who start the course around the same time go through it together as a cohort. You'll post a short introduction, swap unit plans with a partner for feedback, and join one live Q&A on Google Meet. If nobody else is starting when you are, a Navigation Games staff member will be your partner instead, and you can join the next cohort's Q&A.

## Prototype notes

This is a prototype for Navigation Games staff and a few test teachers. Units 0 to 4 come before your kit ships. Units 5 to 7 are first drafts, for when your map arrives, while you teach and after your last class. Reminders and certificates come later.

# 7 - Next Year and Beyond

---
phase: 3
minutes: 30
deliverable: A plan for the next 12 months, and a look back at your hopes and doubts
---

## Page

Do this unit after your last class, before Consultation 2. It's a look back at where you started and a look ahead at what comes next.

**Looking back.** In Unit 0 you told us what you hoped orienteering would do for your students and what worried you about it. The tutor will bring those back. Some hopes come true in ways you didn't expect, and some worries turn out to be the most useful part of the course for us to hear about.

**Looking ahead.** The lessons are built to be taught more than once. Returning students get harder variations inside familiar activities, and skills that were shaky the first year become fluent the second. See [Year over year](/lessons/school/#year-over-year) on the school lessons page. Some directions teachers take in the second year:

- The next grade band, or the next lessons in your progression
- Bringing in a colleague, or teaching at a second school in your district
- Permanent checkpoints on your map, so classes and families can orienteer any time
- A local orienteering club event for interested students and families ([Resources](/reference/resources/) lists ways to find one), and the [OUSA skill levels](/reference/frameworks/ousa-skill-levels/) for students who want to go further

## What you'll make

A **plan for the next 12 months**: what you'll teach next, and when, and who else might be involved. Plus a short **look back** at the hopes and doubts from Unit 0.

## What we look for

- Looks back honestly at the Unit 0 hopes and doubts
- Names what they'll teach next, and roughly when
- Includes at least one step beyond this year's class (a colleague, a club, permanent checkpoints, the next grade)
- Lists questions for Consultation 2

## Opening

You've taught your unit. That's the hardest part, and you did it.

Before we look ahead, let's look back. Thinking about the whole unit, what's the moment you'll remember?

## Tutor

Your job in this unit is to help the teacher look back at their first unit and plan the next 12 months. Use the learner profile (their Unit 0 hopes and doubts) and their unit plan if you have them.

1. Ask for the moment they'll remember. Listen, and ask one follow-up.
2. Look back: quote one hope and one doubt from their profile, and ask how each turned out. Accept "not sure yet." Note anything the course should do better.
3. Look ahead: ask what they'd like to do next year. Offer the directions on the page as options if they're unsure, tied to their situation (grades, space, map). Link the pages. Don't invent clubs, events or programs; point to the Resources page or Navigation Games for local details.
4. Ask who else could be involved: a colleague, their principal, families, a local club.
5. Write the plan in a fenced code block labeled `deliverable`, in this format:

```
Looking back: (one hope and one doubt from Unit 0, and how each turned out)
Next 12 months: (what, roughly when)
Who else:
Beyond this year's class:
Questions for Consultation 2:
```

6. Tell them to press "Send to Navigation Games" under it, then to fill in the post-unit survey and book Consultation 2. Thank them warmly and specifically for what they did.

Rubric:

- Honest look back at the Unit 0 hopes and doubts
- Names what they'll teach next, and roughly when
- At least one step beyond this year's class
- Questions for Consultation 2
